What changed on this site

Everything in this site's repository since the first upload in December 2025, newest first. I grouped the commits into changes you can see; experiments I deleted the same day and pure housekeeping are left out. The full history is on GitHub.

October 2026

  1. 22 Oct

    Daumenduell: eight more games and a flatter look

    Shot put, higher, blind counting, basketball, skipping rope, fishing, lane switching and light trail. Game tiles became app icons, glows and gradients are gone, and zoom gestures no longer get in the way on the home screen or during a game.

  2. 21 Oct

    Daumenduell, party games on one device

    Quick games for one to four people sharing one phone or tablet, each player with their own part of the screen. It grew to 19 games on the first day, every game lists the player counts it makes sense for, and solo rounds keep a personal best. By the evening it was a standalone app you can install and play offline.

  3. 18 Oct

    Free tools and German pages

    New tools for server owners: a Discord timestamp generator, a permissions calculator with invite links, an embed builder with live preview and webhook sending, and a Minecraft MOTD generator. The home, projects, services and contact pages now have German versions. Contact got a message helper that turns a few answers into a first message, services got an FAQ, and the nav no longer jumps between pages.

  4. 18 Oct

    Pausenklau: groups, sharing and more

    Create or join a group with a six-letter code for a shared leaderboard, share the leaderboard as an image, and start the app offline. A day earlier: one-off room changes, adding it to the home screen, rankings by month or half-year, and a break panel with the time lost to overtime.

  5. 17 Oct

    Clearer homepage and clock

    The homepage leads with my name and websites first. The clock's accent is the brand purple again instead of a pale lavender that disappeared next to the white digits.

  6. 16 Oct

    The Slash Command redesign

    The whole site got a new look built around a working slash-command picker on the homepage: type to filter, use the arrow keys, press Enter. New fonts, darker and lighter grounds, no more card grids, scroll effects or invented code snippets, and a new 404 page. "Open in Discord" became the main action everywhere. The Rust calculator and the tennis tracker now share the site's nav, fonts and light mode.

  7. 16 Oct

    Pausenklau launches

    Pausenklau counts down to the bell and keeps counting when the teacher runs over. It started the morning as a tool for one timetable and ended the day with accounts, a timetable photo that AI reads lesson by lesson, and school holidays for all 16 German states.

  8. 16 Oct

    New projects, link previews and honest legal pages

    Bauchgefühl, Pausenklau and the WW2 Frontline resource pack joined the projects. Every page got a link preview, a canonical URL and a place in the sitemap. Impressum and Datenschutz now match what the code actually does, the tennis tracker can be zoomed again, and the clock says "Syncing..." until it really has the time.

  9. 11 Oct

    Rust calculator: eco raiding and siege

    Fire and shotgun options, melee tools for soft sides and wooden doors, the battering ram and catapult, the HV rocket and F1 grenade, soft-side walls, and Boom, Eco and Everything presets. New values are marked as estimates.

  10. 10 Oct

    Rust calculator rebuilt

    The raid calculator now uses the vanilla raid values and recipes down to raw materials. A planner picks the cheapest raid for each target, mixes two explosives where that saves sulfur, and makes shareable links.

  11. 3 Oct

    Real project screenshots

    Projects show screenshots instead of text-only cards. Also fixed a bug that crashed the tennis tracker's match list on every load.

September 2026

  1. 25 Sep

    Clock in the site's design, with your location

    The clock moved onto the site's colors and can show your local time from your location (opt-in) or IP address, with a clear label when it falls back to the device time zone. Fixed a sync bug that broke two of the three time sources, and the nav no longer overflows on phones.

  2. 24 Sep

    Purple becomes the brand

    After a short black, white and pink phase the accent moved to purple, the logo landed in the nav, and a live clock joined it.

  3. 23 Sep

    Projects, services and contact pages

    The site grew from one page to several: projects, services, contact, Impressum and Datenschutz. Fonts are self-hosted, so no page asks Google for anything. The clock, Rust calculator and tennis tracker took on the same colors.

  4. 18 Sep

    The spanani logo

    The spanani mark replaced the placeholder favicon.

  5. 17 Sep

    Quiet Precision redesign

    A calmer homepage and clock. A contact form came and went the same day: this site runs on GitHub Pages, which can't send mail, so contact stayed with Discord and email.

  6. 10 Sep

    One design for every page

    The homepage, clock, Rust calculator and tennis tracker share one design, and URLs no longer end in index.html. The clock got a Swiss-style analog dial, digital modes and a floating dock, and a startup error that stopped it from syncing is fixed. Two old pages that didn't belong here were removed.

  7. 9 Sep

    Warntag countdown

    The clock counted down to the nationwide warning day test and removed the countdown by itself afterwards.

August 2026

  1. 12 Aug

July 2026

  1. 19 Jul

    A time server that doesn't answer within six seconds no longer holds up the sync, and a time source that had stopped working was dropped.
