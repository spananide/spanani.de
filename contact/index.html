Before you message me

I'm in Central European Time and I usually reply within a day. I'm currently taking on new projects.

You'll get a useful answer much faster if your first message includes:

  • What you want built, in plain words — the problem, not the solution
  • Who it's for: a Discord community, a game server, a business, yourself
  • Roughly when you need it, and whether that date is real or aspirational
  • Anything that already exists that it has to work with
